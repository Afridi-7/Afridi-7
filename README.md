## About Me

I design and build **intelligent systems that combine Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and Large Language Models** to solve complex real-world problems — with a focus on **perception, reasoning, and knowledge retrieval**.

Currently pursuing a **BSc in Computer Science** at the University of Debrecen.
